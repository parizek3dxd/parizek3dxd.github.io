FACT ENGINE

Create vertical fact videos in your browser. No account, API key or paid subscription.

Before you start

• Voice preview uses the iPhone's built-in speech engine. No external voice library, API key, or download is needed.

• iOS Safari does not let a webpage export the built-in spoken voice as a WAV track. The preview speaks aloud; exported video contains captions and optional music, not the spoken voice.

• Images and article text come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Check the credits in your video description.

• Keep Safari open and the screen awake while rendering. 60 FPS depends on your iPhone's performance.
