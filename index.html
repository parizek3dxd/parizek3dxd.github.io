FACT ENGINE

A browser-based Shorts maker. No account or API key required.

Before you start

Voice: Kokoro generates English narration on your device. The first download is large (about 90 MB). On iPhone, keep Safari open and the screen awake while it loads and generates.

Images: Wikimedia Commons search, with license credits. You can upload your own images.

Video: Export is recorded in real time. Keep this tab visible until recording finishes. Safari chooses a supported MP4/WebM format automatically.

Choose an audio file you have permission to use.
